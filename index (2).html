How to play

Level:

  • Jump by tapping the screen, clicking, or pressing SPACE — time it to clear the obstacles ahead.
  • Touching an obstacle costs you a life. You start with 3 lives; lose them all and the run ends early.
  • Running into a coin pauses the action and pops up a geometry problem — find the perimeter or area shown in the diagram.
  • You'll answer 10 questions in total, with 45 seconds on the clock for each one.
  • Correct answers add points to your score; your best runs appear on the live leaderboard.
  • Use the 🔊 button (top corner) to mute sound, and "Read Aloud" on any question to hear it spoken.
